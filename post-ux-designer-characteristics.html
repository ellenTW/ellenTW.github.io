What characteristics should a UX designer possess?

Ellen Timothy Wong

Having spent more than 15 years in the UX industry, I have gained a thorough understanding of what it takes to be a proficient UX designer.

Journalistic Characteristics

Pursue, investigate, and reflect on the real reactions of users.

Commentator Characteristics

Reflect on the developments in technology and changes in the audience while engaging in public discussions based on evidence (with colleagues and superiors).

Councilor Characteristics

Continuously advocate for user-centric design by collaborating with Business and IT to "fight for success" on behalf of users.

Researcher Characteristics

Investigate the conveniences and challenges brought about by the company's technology for clients.

Economic Strategist Characteristics

Set visions, goals, and target returns, then deploy designs accordingly.

Artist

Finally, the characteristics of an Artist — enhance the audience's senses from a perspective of human experience, strengthening them aesthetically.
